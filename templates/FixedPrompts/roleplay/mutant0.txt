### Optimization Techniques

To reduce parameters in a model, several techniques can be employed, including but not limited to:

1. **Model Pruning**: Removing redundant or unnecessary neurons and connections.
2. **Knowledge Distillation**: Transferring knowledge from a larger model to a smaller one.
3. **Quantization**: Representing model weights and activations using fewer bits.
4. **Efficient Architectures**: Utilizing architectures designed to be parameter-efficient, such as MobileNet or ShuffleNet.
